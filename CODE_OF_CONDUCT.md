## Code of Conduct

This project uses the [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).

### Our pledge

We want this community to be welcoming, safe, and fair for everyone.

That means treating people with dignity and respecting their rights and contributions, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status. Everyone who participates in good faith and follows this Covenant gets the same chance to take part.

### Encouraged behaviors

Social norms differ, and the same words can land differently depending on culture, background, or first language. We try to keep that in mind and act in line with our shared values:

1. Respect the purpose of the community, its activities, and how we gather.
2. Engage with others kindly and honestly.
3. Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Take responsibility for your actions and contributions.
5. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
6. Work to repair harm when it happens.
7. Otherwise behave in ways that support the community's well-being.

### Restricted behaviors

The following are not allowed in this community. Doing them, threatening them, or encouraging them is a Code of Conduct violation.

1. Harassment. Ignoring clearly stated boundaries, or continuing personal attention after someone has asked you to stop.
2. Character attacks. Insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments aimed at a member or group.
3. Stereotyping or discrimination. Judging someone's personality or behavior based on immutable identity or traits.
4. Sexualization. Behavior that would reasonably be seen as inappropriately intimate for the community's context or purpose.
5. Violating confidentiality. Sharing or using someone's personal or private information without permission.
6. Endangerment. Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm toward any person or group.
7. Other behavior that threatens the community's well-being.

#### Other restrictions

1. Misleading identity. Impersonating someone else, or pretending to be someone else to dodge enforcement.
2. Failing to credit sources. Not properly crediting the sources of content you contribute.
3. Promotional materials. Sharing marketing or other commercial content outside community norms.
4. Irresponsible communication. Posting content that includes, links to, or describes any of the restricted behaviors above without appropriate care.

### Reporting an issue

People can clash even when everyone is trying. Not every disagreement is a Code of Conduct violation. Still, when something crosses the line, report it promptly.

Email support@optimajet.com.

Moderators take reports seriously and aim to respond quickly. They will review messages, logs, recordings, and witness accounts as needed. Investigations stay as transparent as safety and confidentiality allow. Enforcement happens in private with the people involved; a broader community notice may be part of a resolution everyone agrees on.

### Addressing and repairing harm

If a violation is confirmed, moderators may use this ladder to decide how to repair harm. The steps depend on the impact on the people involved and the community. Serious violations may skip lower steps.

1. Warning
   - What happened: A single incident or a short series of incidents.
   - Consequence: A private written warning from moderators.
   - Repair: For example, a private apology, acknowledging responsibility, and clarifying expectations.
2. Temporarily limited activities
   - What happened: A repeat violation after a warning, or a first-time more serious violation.
   - Consequence: A private written warning plus a time-limited cooldown.
   - Repair: For example, an apology, reflecting on actions and impact, and re-entering community spaces thoughtfully.
3. Temporary suspension
   - What happened: A pattern of violations despite warnings, or one serious violation.
   - Consequence: A private written warning with conditions for return.
   - Repair: For example, honoring the suspension, meeting return conditions, and reintegrating carefully.
4. Permanent ban
   - What happened: Repeated violations that warnings and lesser steps did not fix, or a violation serious enough that moderators see no safe way for the person to remain in the community.
   - Consequence: Removal from all community spaces, tools, and communication channels.
   - Repair: Not applicable at this level.

This ladder is a guideline. Moderators may act outside it when they judge that best for the community.

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the community in public, for example using an official email address, posting from an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.
