# Why appearance matters for forms

Confusing layouts and hidden errors can make forms harder to complete. Appearance in Designer includes **readable labels**, **consistent
spacing**, and **visible validation feedback**.

## What you can change

- Field labels, placeholders, and help text (**Main**)
- Colors, fonts, and spacing on components (**Style**)
- Form-wide error and tooltip behavior (**Settings**)

## What to prioritize

1. Legible labels and logical tab order
2. Enough space between sections
3. Error messages users understand (see [Custom error messages](/behavior-and-validation/custom-error-messages))

## Summary

Readable labels, spacing, and clear errors help people finish the form. Start with structure and labels, then refine **Style** after the form works in **Preview**.
