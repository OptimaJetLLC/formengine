# What is FormEngine Designer?

FormEngine Designer is the visual editor for forms and data-entry screens. You pick fields from a library, place them on the canvas, and
set labels, validation, and actions on the right. You do not write application code.

:::info note

This guide shows a typical Designer. Your team can hide tabs, menus, or components in configuration, and some capabilities need an extra
license. If a screenshot or step does not match what you see, ask your developers. They can check setup and licensing. See
[When to involve developers](/advanced-topics/when-to-involve-developers).

:::

## Who it is for

Form owners, analysts, and operations staff use it to lay out fields and validation. It is intended for people who prefer a visual editor
to editing JSON.

## What you can build

You can build contact forms, registration forms, surveys, and tabbed layouts. If your app supplies data, you can also build simple data
screens. Browse [examples and use cases](/meet-the-designer/examples-and-use-cases) for forms you can open in the live demo.

## What you do not need

You do not need React, npm, or a local install when the editor is hosted or embedded. Skip the [API reference](docs:api-reference/) for
everyday layout.

Your developers may embed Designer in an application. These pages cover the UI you see, not the integration code.
