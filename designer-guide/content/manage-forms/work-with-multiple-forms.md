# Work with multiple forms

The **Forms** tab lists forms available through the configured storage. You can search, open, create, and remove forms without exporting
files.

:::info note

If you do not see **Forms**, your host app may use a single-form or file-based workflow. Integrators can hide the **Forms** tab through
configuration. Use [Save, export, and import](/manage-forms/save-export-and-import-forms) when storage is disabled.

:::

## Open the Forms tab

1. Click **Forms** on the left (fourth tab after **Components**, **Tree**, **Settings**).

<img src="/assets/guide/manage-forms/highlight-forms-list-multiple.png" alt="Forms tab listing EventRegistration, ShippingColumns, FeedbackSurvey, and LabelsDemo." />

2. If the list is empty, save the current form first. The message reads: *Save the form so that it appears in the list of forms.*

## Search

1. Type in **Search...** at the top of the tab.
2. The list filters by form name.

<img src="/assets/guide/manage-forms/highlight-forms-search.png" alt="Forms tab with Search filled to Feedback and only FeedbackSurvey shown." />

## Open another form

<img src="/assets/guide/manage-forms/highlight-forms-edit-button.png" alt="Forms tab with arrow pointing at the Edit button on a form row." />

1. Find the form name in the list.
2. Click the **Edit** (pencil) control on that row.
3. The canvas loads that form. The active name is highlighted.

## Add a new form

<img src="/assets/guide/manage-forms/highlight-header-menu-add-new-form.png" alt="Header menu open with Add a new form highlighted." />

1. Click **Add a new form** on the **Forms** tab or in the header **Menu**.
2. Enter a name when prompted.
3. Build on the empty canvas.

## Remove a form

<img src="/assets/guide/manage-forms/highlight-forms-remove-button.png" alt="Forms tab with arrow pointing at the Remove button on a form row." />

1. Click **Remove** on the row you no longer need.
2. Confirm if the app asks. Removed forms cannot be recovered unless you have a backup export.

## Summary

The **Forms** tab is your multi-form library when storage is enabled. **Search...**, **Edit**, **Add a new form**, and **Remove** control
the list. Export backups before bulk cleanup.
